About

Qara's cherries. Germany's habits.

Karaz — كرز — is Arabic for cherry, the fruit our city, Qara, has grown for generations. That's where the name comes from.

Karaz Dev is a small studio based in Qara, Syria. Our engineering habits come from somewhere else — Germany's tech industry, where code review, testing, and performance budgets are just how software gets built.

We brought that back home, with one addition that industry rarely has to think about: most people using what we build are on a connection that drops. We design for that from the start.
